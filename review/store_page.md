# Inner Sun (working title)

The Earth is hollow. Something burns at its heart. Nobody who has gone down to look has come back to say what.

You command a drill ship boring through the crust, a cramped cutaway of decks, bulkheads and nervous crew. Every meter down, the heat climbs. Pipes burst, compartments flood, fires break out two rooms from the reactor, and you decide who runs where, which door to seal, and who gets left behind.

An FTL-style roguelite with a pulp conspiracy streak: sealed crates, suspicious briefings and a company that may know more than it admits. Every dive is different. Most do not end well.

Early prototype build. Art, sound and balance are placeholders.
