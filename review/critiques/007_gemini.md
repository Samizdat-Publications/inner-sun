# Player Review

## Gut reaction
This hits my sweet spot for systems-heavy management, feeling very much like FTL but digging straight down into the crust. The atmosphere is suitably gritty, and the lighting on the drill head looks fantastic as it chews through rock and throws sparks. However, the UI is a dense, overwhelming spreadsheet of orange text, and the ship interiors are so visually noisy it's hard to tell what's going on at a glance. I want to love it, but right now it feels like I'm fighting the interface more than the earth itself.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [ruins it] The giant red "DRILL HAS NO POWER" popup box (00:00 to 00:04 in video). It's massive, blocks the view of the drill head, and is extremely obnoxious. I want this moved to a smaller, contextual UI element near the power bar or the drill itself.
2. [hurts] The UI is overwhelmingly dense. The side panels (seen in the first image) are packed with tiny orange text, and "The Lamp Room" shop (fourth image) is literally just a spreadsheet. I want visual icons, better spacing, and less reliance on raw text.
3. [hurts] Visual noise inside the ship. Every room is packed with pipes, vents, and grates. Without the text labels ("REACTOR", "WEAPONS"), I wouldn't be able to tell the rooms apart at a glance. I want distinct color palettes or iconic central machinery for each room.
4. [nitpick] Crew readability. They blend perfectly into the dark, busy backgrounds, which is why they have those bright colored circles under their feet. I want the character sprites themselves to pop more against the environment.
5. [nitpick] The rock textures outside the hull. As the ship descends (visible throughout the video), the rock wall looks like a very obvious repeating tile pattern, which breaks the immersion of digging through organic, chaotic crust.

## Working, don't touch
- The particle effects, lighting, and sparks on the drill head as it cuts through rock.
- The FTL-style node map (Seismic Survey) for route planning.
