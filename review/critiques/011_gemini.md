# Player Review

Dude, I'm checking out this game that's basically FTL but you're drilling into the Earth. The vibe is incredibly gritty and industrial, like you can feel the heat radiating off the drill. The UI is a massive, overwhelming terminal right now that boxes in the screen, but the core loop of managing power, putting out fires, and fighting rival rigs is totally hooking me.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [ruins it] UI clutter. The HUD panels (Power, Scanner, Crew) form a massive border that suffocates the actual game view (Frame 1). I want the UI scaled down or retractable so the ship is the focus.
2. [hurts] Font readability. The narrow, all-caps orange pixel font used for everything (like the "THIS DIG" tooltip in Frame 1) is a chore to read in large blocks. Give me a cleaner font for secondary information.
3. [hurts] Fire visibility. When the Bridge catches fire (video 3:44), the flames are tiny and blend right into the room's warm color palette. I need bright, high-contrast fire sprites that scream "danger" without needing to read the bottom alert bar.
4. [nitpick] Rigid digging. When breaking into the cavern (video 0:35), the rock vanishes in perfectly square chunks. It looks cheap compared to the ship art. I want crumbling rocks or smoother transitions.
5. [nitpick] Combat impacts. Hitting the rival rig (video 3:30) just produces a tiny spark. I want screen shake, chunkier debris, or flashier explosions to make hits feel weighty.

## Working, don't touch
- The ship interior art and lighting. The rooms are instantly recognizable and the glow effects (like the hot drill and blue reactor) set a perfect mood.
- The FTL-style node map (Frame 4). It's clean, intuitive, and does exactly what it needs to do.
