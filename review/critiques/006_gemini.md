# Player Review

## Gut reaction
I absolutely love the pitch of "FTL but you're in a drill ship going to the center of the Earth." The core systems are all clearly here: routing power, managing a stressed crew, putting out fires, and navigating a node map. But the presentation is incredibly rough right now. It feels exactly like an early prototype, with overwhelming UI and placeholder graphics that make it hard to stay engaged for long. I want to play the finished version of this, but right now it's a chore to look at.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [ruins it] The rock you are drilling through looks like a scrolling 2D texture of wavy sand (video 00:00-01:40). It completely breaks the illusion of a massive drill biting into the earth's crust. I want chunks of rock flying, sparks, and a sense of grinding resistance.
2. [hurts] The UI is a wall of tiny, dense text in a uniform orange/green color scheme (Frames 1-4). It's exhausting to parse during a crisis. I want clean icons, larger fonts for critical info, and less raw data dumped on the screen at once.
3. [hurts] Hazards lack visual impact. When the drill room catches fire and floods (video 01:05), it just gets a light blue tint and some basic particle sparks. I want roaring flames and water visibly filling the space, obscuring the machinery.
4. [nitpick] The crew are tiny, stiff sprites that just slide across the floor (video 01:05-01:20). They don't look like they are fighting for their lives in a tin can. I want expressive animations: actually wrenching on pipes, panicking, or bracing for impact.
5. [nitpick] The rooms are visually noisy boxes of pipes and dials (Frames 1-3). Without the text labels, I couldn't easily tell the Weapons room from the Coolant room. I want distinct color palettes or strong silhouettes for the key machinery in each room.
6. [nitpick] Combat feels disconnected. Firing weapons at the "Rival Rig" (video 03:40) across an empty void of rock looks weird. It doesn't feel like two massive machines clashing underground.

## Working, don't touch
- The core FTL-style gameplay loop of managing systems, crew, and hazards is immediately understandable and compelling.
- The power allocation system (the orange blocks on the left) is clear and easy to read at a glance.
- The node-based map structure is familiar and functional.
