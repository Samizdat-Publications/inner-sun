# Player Review

## Gut reaction
It is literally FTL but you are drilling to the center of the Earth. The core vibe is super claustrophobic and cool, especially with that giant glowing drill at the bottom of the screen carrying the whole aesthetic. But right now it feels like a direct UI and mechanical clone that hasn't fully figured out its own identity, and clicking on passing rocks to mine them feels like a mobile game minigame. If they can nail the subterranean emergencies it will be amazing, but the execution is a bit stiff and visually disjointed right now.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [ruins it] The hazards look incredibly cheap. In the video at 03:23, the fire in the Drill Room is just a pixelated flame overlay that doesn't cast light or interact with the room's visuals. I want dynamic lighting from the flames and smoke filling the upper compartments.
2. [hurts] The world outside the ship is a boring treadmill. It's just a flat rock texture scrolling upwards with zero depth. I want to see background layers, buried structures, or giant caverns passing behind the ship to make it feel like a real descent.
3. [hurts] The art styles are fighting each other. You have a high-resolution, painted-looking UI, pre-rendered 3D-looking crew members, and chunky pixel-art particle effects (like the sparks off the drill). Pick one cohesive style and stick to it.
4. [nitpick] The resource collection feels out of place. Clicking on the copper and iron veins as they scroll by (video at 01:53) disrupts the tension of managing the ship. It feels like a distraction rather than a core system.
5. [nitpick] The crew look completely disconnected from the ship. When Okafor is manning the Reactor (video at 00:00), he just stands in the middle of the floor with an MMO-style selection circle under him. Make them snap to the actual consoles and pull levers.
6. [nitpick] The UI text is unnecessarily hard to read. The typewriter font used in the "Seismic Scanner" and "The Lamp Room" shop screen (video at 03:25) is tiny and strains the eyes against the dark backgrounds.

## Working, don't touch
- The exact copy of the FTL UI layout. Don't fix what isn't broken; it makes the learning curve zero for anyone who plays this genre.
- The concept of the drill bit wearing down and the drill taking damage as a core mechanic. It gives a great sense of inevitable forward momentum.
